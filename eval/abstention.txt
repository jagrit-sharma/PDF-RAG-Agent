Q: Are our risk materiality ratings genuinely driving proportionate controls, or are they a label applied after the fact?
==============================================================================
The passages cannot tell you which is true of your firm — that is an internal evidence question — but they set out what a genuinely driving assessment looks like, giving you testable criteria.

The methodology should be applied **consistently** across every AI use case, system or model, with higher-materiality items receiving more stringent scrutiny [1]. Materiality should map to the **depth and scope of validation and monitoring** required [2], and to control intensity in specific domains — for example, greater attention to bias controls in credit decisioning or insurance underwriting [3].

Two further tests: the rating should be used **before deployment**, to confirm residual risk meets risk appetite, and both the methodology and individual assessments should be **regularly reviewed** for continued relevance [5]. Assessments should also aggregate into an overall AI risk exposure feeding policy revision [4].

If ratings are assigned without changing validation depth, pre-deployment approval, or control selection, they are functioning as labels rather than calibration [1][2].
